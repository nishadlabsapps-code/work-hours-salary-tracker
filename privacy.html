Privacy Policy

Work Hours & Salary Tracker (My Work Hour Attendance)

Effective Date: 25-10-2026

Work Hours & Salary Tracker (also known as "My Work Hour Attendance") ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a) Personal Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Google account identifier and basic profile information made available during Google Sign-In, where applicable.
  • Your email address is used to identify your account and associate your saved app data with it.

b) User Data (Provided by You)

  • Attendance records (date, time, hours, overtime)
  • Salary and work-related information
  • Workplace details
  • Profile image, if uploaded
  • Multiple profiles and their individual settings.
  • Profile information and attendance records associated with each profile, as applicable.

c) Device & Usage Information

  • Device type and OS version
  • App usage behavior
  • Crash logs and diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics
  • Crash reports may include technical diagnostic information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash details, and relevant technical identifiers or app-state information, depending on the diagnostic event and configuration.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide attendance tracking and salary calculation features
  • Store and sync your data using Firebase
  • Display and manage your profile information
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Show advertisements through third-party ad networks, where enabled
  • Associate saved attendance records and profiles with the relevant signed-in account.
  • Allow users to create, edit, manage, and delete multiple profiles.
  • Upload, store, and display profile images using Firebase Storage.
  • Process crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix technical problems.

3. Advertising

We may display ads in the app using third-party services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising ID
  • Usage data

This data may be used to deliver ads, measure advertising performance, and personalize advertising where permitted by applicable settings and policies.

Learn more: Google advertising technologies and privacy.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your device and/or on Firebase servers.
  • Profile images are stored using Firebase Storage.
  • We take reasonable steps to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.
  • Firebase Realtime Database stores attendance records, profile information, and related work data associated with the relevant user account.
  • Saved data may be organized under the user's Google account email address or account identifier within Firebase.
  • Profile images uploaded by users are stored using Firebase Storage.
  • When a user deletes a profile, the associated data is intended to be deleted from Firebase according to the app's deletion functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, as applicable to the features enabled in the app:

  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Firebase for database, storage, and crash reporting
  • Google AdMob for advertising, where enabled

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Firebase services are used for account authentication, cloud data storage and synchronization, profile image storage, and crash diagnostics, as applicable to the features you use. These services process information according to their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Read more: Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy and security information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Data may be shared or processed:

  • With Firebase services for app functionality
  • With advertising providers for showing ads, where enabled

Information is processed by Firebase and other listed service providers as needed to provide app features, maintain functionality, or provide advertising where enabled. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

Google Sign-In information, including your Google account email address and profile information, is not sold or shared with advertising providers for advertising purposes. Advertising providers may separately collect device identifiers and advertising-related information as described in Section 3.

7. Your Choices & Control

You can:

  • Use the app without logging in, where guest mode is available
  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data. This does not necessarily delete information already stored on Firebase servers.
  • Edit or delete individual profiles using the available app features.
  • Request assistance with deleting account-associated information by contacting us at nishadlabs.apps@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time.

Changes will be reflected within this page.

The "Last Updated" date will be revised whenever material changes are made to this Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at:

Email: nishadlabs.apps@gmail.com
